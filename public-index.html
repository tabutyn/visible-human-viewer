This viewer shows a real human cadaver.

Photographic cross-sections and 3D reconstructions show exposed tissue, internal organs, and anatomical nudity. These are real donated human bodies, not illustrations. The images may be disturbing.

Experimental educational and research software. Alignment may be inaccurate and anatomy may be missing. Not for diagnosis, treatment, or surgical planning.

Self-confirmation only. No birth date or identity information is collected.

Viewer controls

Touch: one finger rotates the camera; two fingers pan and pinch to zoom.

Mouse: drag to rotate; Shift-drag to pan; scroll to zoom. Ctrl-drag tilts the plane; Command-drag moves its depth.

Keyboard: W/A/S/D move the camera; Q/E move vertically.

Azimuth and inclination orient the cross-section plane. Depth moves it from −1 to 1 through the volume.
